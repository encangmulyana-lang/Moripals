FAQ

Is my first mint really free?

Yes. Every wallet's first pal is free, with no pool cap. Mint #2–10 costs 0.00015 BNB, #11–20 costs 0.0004 BNB, #21–30 costs 0.0008 BNB.

How does the forge work?

Burn 3 Commons to forge 1 Uncommon, 2 Uncommons to 1 Rare, 2 Rares to 1 Epic, 2 Epics to 1 Legendary, 2 Legendaries to 1 Mythic. The first 100 forges are gas-only; after that each forge burns 1,000 $MORP.

What is $MORP?

$MORP is the forest token. Each pal claims 10,000 $MORP once, for a fee of 0.00016 BNB per pal. Cap 200M.

When does v3 open?

This is a preview — the v3 contracts are not deployed yet. v2 (257 minted) pauses when v3 launches.
