04 · Docs

How it works.

  1. Connect your wallet (BNB Chain). Your first mint is free while 500 free mints last.
  2. Pick a quantity (1–10). The site shows your exact total — your first pal is free, the rest cost 0.0002 BNB each.
  3. Confirm in your wallet. Your pal is a random draw from the six forest friends.
  4. No browser wallet? Use the agent prompt above, or open this site inside a wallet app browser.
